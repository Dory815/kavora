Cirrus relative-density atlas
=============================

textures/cirrus_density.dds contains scalar cloud morphology extracted from
three photographs released by their authors under CC0 1.0 Universal.

Modifications: four small cloud groups are cropped, their local blue-sky
colour gradient and camera noise floor are removed, and foreground leaves
are excluded. One scalar relative-density channel is packed into a 1024 x
1024 R8_UNORM DDS with eleven mip levels. The source colour, sun direction,
landscape and lens flare are not stored. Source bounds and processing are
recorded in packaging/runtime-textures/cirrus_sources.json and
prepare_cirrus_density.py. Original photographs are not shipped.

The result is an authored morphology field, not a measurement of optical
depth or a recovery of cloud microphysics. The renderer supplies extinction,
height profile, atmospheric illumination and scattering in its own pipeline.
No Photon code or texture is included.

The derived density image is also made available under CC0 1.0 Universal.
The conversion script retains the source license stated in that file.
